# Problem Statement

Many small milk tea and coffee shops still use manual order taking and handwritten receipts. This can lead to slow service, incorrect orders, inaccurate inventory records, and difficulty tracking daily sales.

Teamtea POS Version 2 is designed to make ordering, payment processing, inventory management, and sales reporting faster, more accurate, and easier for both employees and business owners.

---

# Project Goal

To develop a modern Point of Sale (POS) system that helps milk tea and coffee shops efficiently manage customer orders, inventory, payments, and sales reports while providing faster customer service.

---

# Objectives

* Speed up customer ordering and payment.
* Reduce order errors.
* Automatically calculate bills and change.
* Monitor product inventory.
* Generate daily, weekly, and monthly sales reports.
* Improve customer satisfaction through faster service.
